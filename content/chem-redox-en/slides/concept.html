ALWAYS A PAIR

One loses oxygen, another gains it

2CuO + C → 2Cu + CO₂

CuO loses oxygen → it is reduced to copper.

C gains oxygen → it is oxidized to CO₂.

Oxidation and reduction always happen at the same time.

Oxidation & Reduction · 5

Trace the oxygen atom with your finger: it starts on copper and ends on carbon. Whatever the oxygen leaves is reduced; whatever it joins is oxidized.
