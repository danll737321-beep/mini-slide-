Core Concept

1 Gain or lose oxygen

Gain oxygen = oxidation
Lose oxygen = reduction

2 Always a pair

When one substance loses oxygen, another gains it, at the same time.

3 The agents

The reducing agent takes oxygen and is itself oxidized.

4 Reactivity series (most → least)

K > Na > Ca > Mg > Al > C > Zn > Fe > Sn > Pb > H > Cu > Hg > Ag > Pt > Au

Smelting: Fe₂O₃ + 3CO → 2Fe + 3CO₂ (carbon is more reactive than iron, so it takes the oxygen)

L3 · Core Concept · 2–7 min

A more reactive element can replace a less reactive one in its compound. Link back to the hook: iron is more reactive than copper, so copper is pushed out of the solution.
