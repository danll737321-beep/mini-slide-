BEFORE YOU START

Four words you need first

Oxidation

A substance gains oxygen. Iron rusting is oxidation.

Reduction

A substance loses oxygen. Getting iron from ore is reduction.

Oxidizing agent

Gives its oxygen away (and is reduced).

Reducing agent

Takes oxygen from something else (and is oxidized).
