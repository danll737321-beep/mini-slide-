THE MOST COMMON MYTH

Is rusting just a color change?

No.

Rust (iron oxide) is a new substance: flaky, weak and orange-brown. That makes rusting a chemical change, and an oxidation.

A rusty nail actually gets heavier, because it has gained oxygen atoms from the air.

Contrast with a physical change such as bending a nail or melting ice: no new substance forms.
