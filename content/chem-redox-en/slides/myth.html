True or False?

1　“Oxidation and reduction are two separate reactions.”

2　“The more reactive a metal is, the more easily it is reduced.”

3　“Rusting is only a color change, so it is a physical change.”

Explain what’s wrong with each statement.

L3 · Misconceptions · 7–11 min

All three are false. 1: Oxidation and reduction always happen together. 2: A more reactive metal is more easily oxidized, and its oxide is harder to reduce. 3: Rust (iron oxide) is a new substance, so rusting is a chemical change and an oxidation.
