RUSTING

Iron needs both air and water to rust

Only the left nail rusts.

Rusting is slow oxidation: iron + oxygen + water → rust (iron oxide).

Take away air or water and rusting stops.

Paint, oil and zinc coatings keep oxygen and water away.
