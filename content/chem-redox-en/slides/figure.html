REACTIVITY SERIES

Some elements grab oxygen harder than others

K > Na > Ca > Mg > Al > C > Zn > Fe > Sn > Pb > H > Cu > Hg > Ag > Pt > Au

A more reactive element can take oxygen away from a less reactive one.

Carbon sits above iron, so carbon can pull oxygen out of iron ore.

Gold and platinum hardly react, which is why they are found as pure metal.
