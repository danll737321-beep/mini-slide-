Pair Practice

Q1

2CuO + C → 2Cu + CO₂

Which substance is oxidized, which is reduced, and which is the reducing agent?

Q2

Cu + FeSO₄ → ?

Will a copper strip react with iron(II) sulfate solution? Why or why not?

L3 · Practice · 11–14 min

Q1: C gains oxygen, so it is oxidized and is the reducing agent; CuO loses oxygen, so it is reduced and is the oxidizing agent. Q2: No reaction. Copper is less reactive than iron, so it cannot replace iron.
