THE MOST COMMON MYTH

After 3 purple plants, must the 4th be white?

No.

Every offspring is a new chance. Each one has its own 1 in 4 chance of being white, no matter what came before.

It’s like flipping a coin: three heads in a row doesn’t make the fourth flip more likely to be tails.

Genetics: Dominant & Recessive · 12

A 3 : 1 ratio is what you approach over many offspring, not a guarantee for every group of four.
