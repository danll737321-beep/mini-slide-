COMMON MISCONCEPTION

Every offspring is a new chance

Misconception

“After 3 purple plants, the 4th one must be white.”

Correct

Each offspring has its own 1 in 4 chance of being white.

Genetics: Dominant & Recessive · 5

Analogy: after flipping heads three times in a row, the fourth flip is still 50/50. A 1:2:1 ratio is what you approach over many offspring, not a guarantee for every group of four. This leads straight into the coin activity.
