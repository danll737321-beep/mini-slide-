VOCABULARY · 2–4 MIN

Capital A = dominant, lowercase a = recessive

gene

A unit of heredity that controls a trait

dominant (A)

Shows up when at least one copy is present

recessive (a)

Shows up only with two copies (aa)

genotype / phenotype

The letters, like Aa / The trait you can see, like purple

Genetics: Dominant & Recessive · 3

Stress the notation: a capital letter is dominant, the same letter in lowercase is recessive. Genotype is what you write; phenotype is what you see.
