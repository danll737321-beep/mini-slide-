MIDDLE SCHOOL BIOLOGY MINI-LESSON 2/3

Genetics: Dominant & Recessive Traits

Predicting offspring with the Punnett square

15 minutes · Grade 7 · Unit: Heredity

Objective: Students complete an Aa × Aa Punnett square and find the 1:2:1 genotype and 3:1 phenotype ratios.

This is the highest-priority lesson of the three: heavily tested and full of misconceptions. Prep: 2 coins per pair, labeled with stickers (heads = A, tails = a); one tally sheet per pair; an empty 2×2 grid drawn on the board.
