Two questions, on your own

1

The father is Aa and the mother is aa. What is the chance of a recessive offspring? Draw the Punnett square.

2

Why didn’t 20 flips give exactly 1:2:1, while the class total came closer?

Genetics: Dominant & Recessive · 8

Answers: 1. 1/2 (Aa : aa = 2 : 2). 2. Probability only gets close to the expected ratio with a large number of trials. Students who answer 1/4 on question 1 probably reused the Aa × Aa result; next time, have them draw the square before calculating.
