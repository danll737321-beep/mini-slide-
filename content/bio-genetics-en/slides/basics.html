BEFORE YOU START

Four words you need first

Gene

An instruction for one trait, like flower color. You get two copies, one from each parent.

Dominant (A)

Shows up if you have at least one copy. Written as a capital letter.

Recessive (a)

Shows up only with two copies (aa). Written in lowercase.

Genotype / phenotype

The letters (Aa) / the trait you can see (purple).

Genetics: Dominant & Recessive · 4

Stress the notation: a capital letter is dominant, the same letter in lowercase is recessive. Genotype is what you write; phenotype is what you see.
