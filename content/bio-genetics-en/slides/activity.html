ACTIVITY · 8–13 MIN · PAIRS

Flip coins to model gametes

  1. One partner is the father, one is the mother. Heads = A, tails = a.
  2. Flip at the same time and record the pair.
  3. Repeat 20 times.
  4. Add up the class totals and compare with 1:2:1.

Genetics: Dominant & Recessive · 6

Remind students that A + a and a + A both count as Aa. In the last minute, collect each pair’s counts on the board and total them. One pair’s 20 flips often misses 1:2:1; the class total of several hundred flips comes closer. This sets up exit ticket question 2.
