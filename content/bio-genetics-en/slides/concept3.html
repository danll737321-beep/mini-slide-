HIDDEN GENES

Same look, different letters

You can’t tell them apart by looking.

AA and Aa are both purple: same phenotype, different genotype.

An Aa plant is a carrier: it hides the white gene and can pass it on.

To find out, cross it with a white (aa) plant and see if any white flowers appear.

Genetics: Dominant & Recessive · 9

This is called a test cross. If the purple parent is AA, all offspring are purple; if it is Aa, about half are white.
