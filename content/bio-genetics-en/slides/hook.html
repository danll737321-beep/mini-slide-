Can two purple-flowered pea plants have a white-flowered offspring?

Vote: yes or no?

Genetics: Dominant & Recessive · 2

Show a photo or sketch of pea flowers if you have one. Take the vote and write the tally on the board without giving the answer. The answer is yes: if both purple parents are Aa, each offspring has a 1 in 4 chance of being white (aa). Avoid examples like eyelid folds or tongue rolling; they are controlled by several genes, not a simple dominant–recessive pair.
