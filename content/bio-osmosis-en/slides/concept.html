DIFFUSION

Particles spread out until they’re even

A drop of food coloring in still water.

Particles are always jiggling and bumping.

Over time they move from where they’re crowded (high concentration) to where they’re spread out (low concentration).

No push needed: diffusion just happens.

Cells: Osmosis · 5

Quick demo: one drop of food coloring in a clear cup of still water, or spray air freshener at the front of the room and ask students to raise a hand when they smell it.
