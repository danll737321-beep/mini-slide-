BEFORE YOU START

Four words you need first

Cell membrane

The thin skin around a cell. Water passes through; salt hardly does.

Concentration

How much stuff (like salt) is in a set amount of water.

Diffusion

Particles spreading from crowded to less crowded.

Osmosis

Diffusion of water across a membrane.

Cells: Osmosis · 4

Point out that osmosis is a special case of diffusion: same idea, but only water is moving.
