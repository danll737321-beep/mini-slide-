Compare the two potato strips

  1. Take one strip from each cup.
  2. Measure each strip with a ruler.
  3. Gently bend each one. Which is firm? Which is soft?
  4. Decide: which strip gained water, and which lost water?

The strips must soak for at least 40 minutes beforehand (3 in plain water, 3 in 10% salt water). Expected results: the plain-water strip is firm and slightly longer (gained water); the salt-water strip is soft, bends easily and is slightly shorter (lost water).
