Why does salted cucumber get watery?

Salt on cucumber

Ten minutes later, water pools on the plate and the slices go limp.

Water on lettuce

Stores spray vegetables so they stay crisp and firm.

In both cases, water is moving in or out of the vegetable’s cells.
