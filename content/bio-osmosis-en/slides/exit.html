Two questions, on your own

1

A red blood cell is placed in pure water. Which way does the water move? What happens to the cell?

2

Why do grocery stores spray water on fresh vegetables?

Cells: Osmosis · 8

Answers: 1. Water moves into the cell; the cell swells and may burst. 2. There is more water outside, so water moves into the cells and the vegetables stay crisp. Students who miss both: repeat the cucumber demo at the start of next class.
