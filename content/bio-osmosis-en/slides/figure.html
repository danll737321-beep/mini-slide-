OSMOSIS

Which way does the water go?

Black dots = salt. Blue arrows = which way water moves.

Pure water outside: the cell is saltier, so water moves in.

Same saltiness: water moves both ways equally, so nothing changes.

Salt water outside: the outside is saltier, so water moves out.

Cells: Osmosis · 6

Draw one cell on the board and add salt dots inside and outside; ask students to predict the arrows before revealing.
