ANIMAL VS. PLANT CELLS

Too much water can burst a red blood cell

Red blood cells in three liquids.

Animal cells have only a thin membrane, so too much water can make them burst.

Plant cells have a stiff cell wall, so they get firm instead of bursting. That’s why sprayed lettuce is crisp.

That’s why IV drips use saline (salt water), never pure water.

Cells: Osmosis · 9

Saline is about 0.9% salt, the same saltiness as blood, so water moves in and out equally.
