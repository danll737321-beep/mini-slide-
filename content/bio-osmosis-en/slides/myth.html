COMMON MISCONCEPTION

The water moves, not the salt

Misconception

“The salt moves into the cell.”

Correct

The membrane lets water through. Salt does not cross easily.

Cells: Osmosis · 5

Students often read “more salt” as “the salt moves.” Close it with one line: the salt stays put; the water is what moves. If time is short, cover this orally during the concept section and skip the slide.
