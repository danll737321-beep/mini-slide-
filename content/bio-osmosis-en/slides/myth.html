THE MOST COMMON MYTH

Does the salt move into the cell?

No.

The membrane lets water through easily, but salt hardly crosses. The salt stays put; the water is what moves.

That’s why salted cucumber gets watery instead of salty inside: water comes out to the salt.

Cells: Osmosis · 12

Students read “more salt” as “the salt moves.” Close it with one line: the salt stays; the water travels.
