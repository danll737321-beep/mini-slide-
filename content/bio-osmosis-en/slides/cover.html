BIOLOGY

Cells: Osmosis

How water moves in and out of cells

Objective: Students predict which way water moves across a cell membrane and explain it using concentration.

Prep: At least 40 minutes before class, soak 6 equal potato strips: 3 in plain water and 3 in 10% salt water. A 15-minute soak in class will not show a difference. Also bring cucumber slices, salt, rulers and paper towels.
