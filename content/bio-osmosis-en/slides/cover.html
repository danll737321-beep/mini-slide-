MIDDLE SCHOOL BIOLOGY MINI-LESSON 1/3

Cells: Osmosis

How water moves in and out of cells

15 minutes · Grade 7 · Unit: Cells, the Basic Unit of Life

Objective: Students predict which way water moves across a cell membrane and explain it using concentration.

Prep: At least 40 minutes before class, soak 6 equal potato strips: 3 in plain water and 3 in 10% salt water. A 15-minute soak in class will not show a difference. Also bring cucumber slices, salt, rulers and paper towels. Timing: Hook 2 min, Vocabulary 2 min, Concept 4 min, Activity 5 min, Exit ticket 2 min.
