VOCABULARY · 2–4 MIN

Four words to keep on the board

cell membrane

The thin layer that controls what enters and leaves a cell

diffusion

Particles moving from high concentration to low concentration

osmosis

The diffusion of water across a cell membrane

concentration

How much of a substance (like salt) is in a given amount of water

Cells: Osmosis · 3

Students copy the four words into their notes. Point out that osmosis is a special case of diffusion: same idea, but only water is moving.
