BEFORE YOU START

Four words you need first

Reactant

What you start with. In CH₄ + O₂, both are reactants.

Product

The new substances that form, written after the arrow.

Coefficient

The big number in front: 2H₂O means two water molecules.

Subscript

The small number inside: H₂O has two H atoms.
