True or False?

1　“A candle gets lighter as it burns, so mass is not conserved.”

2　“To balance H₂ + O₂ → H₂O, just change the product to H₂O₂.”

3　“The ratio of coefficients is the ratio of masses.”

Explain what’s wrong with each statement.

Chemical Reactions & Conservation of Mass · 5

All three are false. 1: The CO₂ and H₂O produced escape into the air. 2: Changing a subscript makes a different substance; the correct equation is 2H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O. 3: Coefficients give the ratio of moles, not masses.
