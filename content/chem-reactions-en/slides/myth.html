THE MOST COMMON MYTH

A candle gets lighter as it burns. Is mass lost?

No.

The wax reacts with oxygen and turns into CO₂ and water vapor. Both are gases, so they float away and the scale can’t see them.

Burn a candle inside a sealed jar on a scale and the reading stays the same. The atoms are all still there.

The opposite surprise: steel wool gets heavier when it burns, because it gains oxygen from the air. Both cases follow the same rule.
