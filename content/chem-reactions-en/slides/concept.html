WHAT A REACTION REALLY IS

Atoms only change partners

Count them: 1 C, 4 H, 4 O on each side.

In a reaction, atoms are not created or destroyed.

They break apart and join up in new ways, making new substances.

Same atoms before and after → same total mass.
