Core Concept

1 Atoms rearrange

Atoms are not created or destroyed, so total mass stays the same.

2 Why Group A got lighter

CO₂ gas escaped into the air. The mass did not disappear.

3 Change coefficients, not subscripts

A new subscript makes a different substance: H₂O → H₂O₂

4 The mole

1 mol = 6 × 10²³ particles
mass (g) = moles × molecular mass
