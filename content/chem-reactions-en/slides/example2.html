WORKED EXAMPLE

How many grams of O₂ are needed to burn 16 g of CH₄?

1

Moles of CH₄

16 ÷ 16
= 1 mol

2

Read the ratio

CH₄ : O₂
= 1 : 2

3

Moles of O₂

1 × 2
= 2 mol

4

Grams of O₂

2 × 32
= 64 g

Answer: 64 g of O₂
