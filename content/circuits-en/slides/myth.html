THE MOST COMMON MYTH

Does a bulb use up current?

No.

In a series circuit the current is the same before and after the bulb. The bulb uses up the electrons’ energy, not the electrons.

Like water turning a waterwheel: the wheel spins, but not one drop is lost. A “dead” battery has run out of energy, not electrons.
