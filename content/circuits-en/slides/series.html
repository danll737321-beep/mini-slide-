WIRING STYLE 1

Series: only one path

The middle bulb broke, so all three went dark.

Bulbs are lined up one after another, like beads on a single string.

One breaks and it’s like cutting the pipe: the whole loop stops.

More bulbs add more resistance, so each bulb gets dimmer.

Example: old holiday lights. (Many newer strings have a bypass so one dead bulb doesn’t kill the rest.)

Circuits & Ohm’s Law · 10

Back to the opening puzzle. In series there is only one path, so the water has to pass through every bulb in turn. When one filament breaks, the whole loop stops flowing and every bulb goes dark.
