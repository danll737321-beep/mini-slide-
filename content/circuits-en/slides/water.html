THINK OF WATER PIPES

A circuit is like a loop of pipe

The pump pushes water around. The narrow part is the bulb.

Water worldCircuit world
PumpBattery
PipeWire
Water in the pipeElectrons

Key idea: wires are already full of electrons. A battery doesn’t make them; it pushes them.

Circuits & Ohm’s Law · 4

Students often think a battery “creates” electricity. Stress that metal wires already hold huge numbers of electrons, just as a pipe is already full of water. The battery only pushes. We come back to this when we bust the myth at the end.
