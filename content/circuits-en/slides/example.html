WORKED EXAMPLE

A 9 V battery is connected to a 3 Ω bulb. What is the current?

1

Write what you know

V = 9 V
R = 3 Ω

2

Pick the formula

We want current, so
I = V ÷ R

3

Plug in and solve

I = 9 ÷ 3
  = 3

4

Add the unit

Current is in amps
3 A

Answer: the current is 3 amps
