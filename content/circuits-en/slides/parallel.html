WIRING STYLE 2

Parallel: many paths

The middle bulb broke; the other two still glow.

The main wire splits into branches, like exits off a highway. Each branch has its own bulb.

If one branch breaks, only that branch stops: the others stay on.

Every branch connects straight to the battery, so each bulb gets the full voltage.

Examples: lights, fridge and TV at home; classroom ceiling lights.

Circuits & Ohm’s Law · 11

Turning off the bedroom light doesn’t stop the fridge. That isn’t automatic; it’s because home appliances are deliberately wired in parallel.
