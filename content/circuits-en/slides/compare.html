Remember: series is “one path, one breaks, all stop.” Parallel is “many paths, each on its own.”

Circuits & Ohm’s Law · 12

Ask students to predict the brightness row first: two identical bulbs on the same battery. In parallel each bulb gets the full voltage; in series they share it, so parallel bulbs are brighter.
