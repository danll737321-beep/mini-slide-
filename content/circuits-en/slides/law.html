OHM’S LAW

V

voltage

=

I

current

×

R

resistance

Need the current? I = V ÷ R (push ÷ blockage = flow)

Why “I” for current? It comes from the French word intensité (intensity). It’s just a tradition.

Circuits & Ohm’s Law · 7

Georg Ohm, a German scientist, wrote this intuition down as a formula about 200 years ago. It only has three letters. The key skill is flipping it: to find current, divide voltage by resistance.
