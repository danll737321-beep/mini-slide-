GUESS FIRST

How do push and blockage change the flow?

Pump pushes harder
voltage goes up

More water flows
current goes up

Pipe gets more clogged
resistance goes up

Less water flows
current goes down

What you just guessed is a law of physics.

Circuits & Ohm’s Law · 6

Ask both questions before revealing: if the pump pushes harder, what happens to the flow? If the pipe gets more clogged? Almost everyone gets these right. That intuition is Ohm’s law.
