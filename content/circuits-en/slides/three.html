The three main characters

V

Voltage

Unit: volt (V)

The push. How hard the pump pushes the water.

An AA battery is 1.5 V. A US wall outlet is about 120 V.

I

Current

Unit: ampere (A)

The flow. How much water passes one point each second.

More flow makes a bulb brighter.

R

Resistance

Unit: ohm (Ω)

The blockage. A narrow or clogged pipe that slows the water.

A bulb is a resistor: its thin filament heats up and glows.

Circuits & Ohm’s Law · 5

One line to remember: voltage is the push, current is the flow, resistance is the blockage. Units: volts, amps, ohms.
