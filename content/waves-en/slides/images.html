Waves, Sound & Light · 13

Memory trick: the closer the object gets to the focal point, the bigger the image; once it is inside the focal length, the image flips to upright and virtual. Look at a word through a magnifier from far to near to see the image go from small and upside down, to large and upside down, to large and upright.
