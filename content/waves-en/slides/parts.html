Three things to know about a wave

Wavelength λ

Unit: meters (m)

The distance from one crest to the next (orange bracket).

Amplitude

How tall the wave is

The height of a crest above the middle (blue line). Bigger = more energy.

Frequency f

Unit: hertz (Hz)

Vibrations per second. 100 Hz means 100 vibrations every second.
