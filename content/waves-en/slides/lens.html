CONVEX LENS

A convex lens brings light to a point

Parallel rays pass through and meet at one point (orange dot). The orange line below is the focal length.

Convex lens: thick in the middle, thin at the edges, like a magnifier.

Focal point F: where parallel rays meet.

Focal length f: from the lens center to the focal point.

Waves, Sound & Light · 12

Outdoors, a magnifier can focus sunlight into a small bright spot: that’s the focal point (never aim it at eyes or anything flammable). Remind students that this f means focal length; it is not the frequency f from the wave-speed formula, just the same letter.
