START WITH A PUZZLE

Why can you hear the next room, but not see into it?

Sound

Slips under doors and bends around corners, so you hear it through a wall.

Light

Travels almost only in straight lines, so a wall blocks it and you can’t see who’s there.

Sound and light are both waves, with different personalities. Let’s meet them.

Waves, Sound & Light · 2

Let students guess. The answer has to do with wavelength: sound waves are tens of centimeters to a few meters long, about the size of doorways and corners, so they bend around them easily. Light’s wavelength is tiny, so it travels almost in straight lines. Come back to this after teaching wavelength.
