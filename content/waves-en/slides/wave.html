WHAT IS A WAVE?

A wave carries energy, not stuff

A stadium wave: the “stand up” moves right, but everyone stays in their own seat.

A wave is a vibration passed along, one neighbor to the next. What travels is energy; the material stays put.

Drop a stone in a pond: ripples spread out, but a leaf on the water just bobs up and down.

Waves, Sound & Light · 4

Have the class do a stadium wave. Each person just stands and sits, never leaving their seat, yet the “stand up” travels across the room. That is a wave: it carries energy, not stuff.
