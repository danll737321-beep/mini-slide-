SOUND

Three properties of sound

PropertySet byEveryday example
LoudnessAmplitude: bigger is louderWhispering vs. shouting
PitchFrequency: higher is shrillerA bird’s chirp (high) vs. a bass drum (low)
TimbreWave shape: different for every instrumentPiano and violin playing the same note still sound different

Human ears hear about 20–20,000 Hz. Higher than that is ultrasound, which dogs and bats can hear.
