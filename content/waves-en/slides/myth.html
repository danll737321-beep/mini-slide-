THE MOST COMMON MYTH

Does a convex lens always magnify?

Not always.

Only when the object is inside the focal length do you see an upright, larger, virtual image. That’s the usual magnifier view.

Look at a faraway window through a magnifier and you see a small, upside-down image, the same way a camera works.

Waves, Sound & Light · 15

A live demo convinces best: have students hold a magnifier at arm’s length and look across the room or out a window. They’ll see a small, upside-down picture.
