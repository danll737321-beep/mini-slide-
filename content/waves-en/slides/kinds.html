TWO SHAPES OF WAVES

Transverse and longitudinal

Transverse

Like shaking a rope: it moves up and down while the wave travels sideways.

Examples: rope waves, light

Longitudinal

Like pushing a spring: it squeezes back and forth in the same direction the wave travels.

Examples: spring waves, sound

Waves, Sound & Light · 7

A Slinky shows both: wiggle it side to side for a transverse wave, push and pull it for a longitudinal wave. Sound is air being squeezed and stretched (longitudinal); light is transverse.
