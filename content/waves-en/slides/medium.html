SOUND NEEDS A MEDIUM

Can you hear an explosion in space?

No.

Sound travels by particles in a medium pushing their neighbors. Space is a vacuum with no particles to push, so sound can’t travel.

Light doesn’t need a medium, which is how sunlight crosses space to reach Earth.

Traveling throughSpeed (about)
Sound in air340 m/s
Sound in water1,500 m/s
Sound in steel5,000 m/s
Light300,000,000 m/s

Light is far faster, which is why you see lightning before you hear thunder.

The “boom” in space movies is a sound effect; real space is silent. Extension: count the seconds between a lightning flash and the thunder, multiply by 340, and you get roughly how many meters away it struck. All speeds are approximate and change with temperature and material.
