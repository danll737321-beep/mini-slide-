Reflection: light bounces

The dashed “normal” line is at right angles to the mirror. The two orange angles match.

Light travels in straight lines, which is why blocked light makes a shadow.

When light hits a smooth surface it bounces off, like a ball off a wall.

Angle in = angle out

Examples: mirrors, reflections on a calm lake, bike reflectors.

Waves, Sound & Light · 10

Shine a flashlight at a mirror and change the angle: the reflected beam moves too, and the two angles always match. Angles are measured from the normal line, not from the mirror surface.
