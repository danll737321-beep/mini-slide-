THE pH SCALE

From 0 to 14: acidic, neutral, basic

Below 7 is acidic, 7 is neutral, above 7 is basic. The further from 7, the stronger.

Below 7: acidic. Lemon juice is about 2.

Exactly 7: neutral. Pure water.

Above 7: basic. Bleach is about 13.

Acids, Bases & Salts · 6

Values are typical; real products vary. Point out that soda is acidic even though it tastes sweet: the sugar hides the sourness.
