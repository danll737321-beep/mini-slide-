BEFORE YOU START

Four words you need first

Acid

Tastes sour. Lemon juice, vinegar and soda are acidic.

Base

Feels slippery. Soap, baking soda and bleach are basic.

pH

A number from 0 to 14 that says how acidic or basic something is.

Indicator

A dye that changes color with pH, like purple cabbage juice.
