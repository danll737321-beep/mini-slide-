START WITH A PUZZLE

Why does purple cabbage juice change color?

Add vinegar

The purple juice turns bright pink.

Add soapy water

The same purple juice turns green.

Same juice, opposite colors. Vinegar and soap belong to two opposite families: acids and bases.

Acids, Bases & Salts · 2

If you can, do it live: boil chopped purple cabbage, strain the juice, and pour it into clear cups. Add vinegar to one and soapy water or baking soda water to another. Ask students to guess before you pour. Optional extension: test soda, lemon juice and window cleaner too, and line the cups up from pink to green.
