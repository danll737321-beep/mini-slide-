Does “salt” just mean table salt?

No.

In chemistry, a salt is any compound made when an acid and a base neutralize each other. Table salt (NaCl) is only one of them.

Baking soda, chalk (calcium carbonate) and many fertilizers are salts too, and most of them don’t taste salty at all.

Link back to neutralization: change the acid or the base and you get a different salt. HCl + KOH makes KCl, a salt substitute.
