True or False?

1　“pH 3 is only a little more acidic than pH 5.”

2　“Salt just means table salt.”

3　“Sugar water conducts electricity because sugar dissolves.”

Explain what’s wrong with each statement.

L2 · Misconceptions · 7–11 min

All three are false. 1: The [H⁺] differs by 100 times. 2: “Salt” is the general name for ionic compounds formed by neutralization; NaCl is only one of them. 3: Dissolving is not the same as splitting into ions; sugar is a non-electrolyte and does not conduct.
