Goals & Key Terms

By the end of class, you can…

  • Identify acids and bases by H⁺ and OH⁻
  • Explain what a change of 1 on the pH scale means
  • Write the products of a neutralization reaction

Materials: red cabbage juice, 4 clear cups, vinegar, soda, soapy water, baking soda water

TermMeaning
electrolytesplits into ions in water
acidreleases H⁺ in water
basereleases OH⁻ in water
pHscale of acidity, 0–14
indicatorchanges color with pH
neutralizationacid + base → salt + water
saltionic product of neutralization
