ELECTROLYTES

Acids, bases and salts conduct electricity

Salt water: the bulb lights up.

In water, acids, bases and salts split into charged particles (ions).

Moving charges carry electricity, so the bulb lights up.

Sugar dissolves but does not split into ions, so sugar water does not conduct.

Acids, Bases & Salts · 9

Substances that conduct in water are called electrolytes. Optional demo: a simple conductivity tester with a 9 V battery and an LED, tried in tap water, salt water, vinegar and sugar water.
