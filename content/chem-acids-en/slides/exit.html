How many times more H⁺ does a pH 2 solution have than a pH 4 solution?

Show how you got your answer.

L2 · Exit Ticket · 14–15 min

Answer: 100 times (a difference of 2 → 10²). Students who answer 2 or 20 times need a quick review of the log scale at the start of next class.
