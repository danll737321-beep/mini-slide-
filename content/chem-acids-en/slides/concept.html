Core Concept

1 Electrolytes

They split into ions in water, so they conduct electricity. Acids, bases and salts are all electrolytes.

2 Acids and bases

Acids release H⁺ in water.
Bases release OH⁻ in water.

3 pH at 25°C

< 7 acidic · = 7 neutral · > 7 basic
Each step of 1 = 10 times the [H⁺]

4 Neutralization

acid + base → salt + water
At its core: H⁺ + OH⁻ → H₂O
