WHAT MAKES AN ACID OR A BASE

Acids give off H⁺. Bases give off OH⁻.

Left: acid in water. Right: base in water.

When an acid dissolves in water, it releases H⁺ (hydrogen ions).

When a base dissolves in water, it releases OH⁻ (hydroxide ions).

More H⁺ → more acidic. More OH⁻ → more basic.

Acids, Bases & Salts · 5

The “+” and “−” mean these particles carry an electric charge; they are called ions. Students do not need the full ion theory yet, only the rule: acids give H⁺, bases give OH⁻.
