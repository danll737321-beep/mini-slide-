Try it

If something eats the wolf, how much energy reaches level 4?

Ecology: Food Webs & Energy Pyramids · 4

Answer: 10 kJ. At each level about 90% is used for breathing and movement and lost as heat, or stays in parts that are not eaten. That is why top predators are the fewest. 10% is an approximation; it is the standard figure in textbooks and tests.
