EXIT TICKET · 13–15 MIN

Two questions, on your own

1

Grass stores 50,000 kJ of energy. Using the 10% rule, how much energy reaches a hawk that eats rabbits (level 3)?

2

Why do food chains rarely have more than 4 or 5 levels?

Ecology: Food Webs & Energy Pyramids · 8

Answers: 1. 500 kJ (50,000 → 5,000 → 500). 2. About 90% of the energy is lost at each level, so the top levels do not have enough energy to support a population. Students who answer 5,000 skipped a level; have them write out all three levels first.
