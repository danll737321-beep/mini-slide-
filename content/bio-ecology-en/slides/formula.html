Only about 10% of the energy passes up each level.

The other 90% is used for living (breathing, moving, keeping warm) and lost as heat, or stays in parts that aren’t eaten.

Ecology: Food Webs & Energy Pyramids · 8

10% is an approximation; real values range from about 5 to 20%. It is the standard figure in textbooks and tests.
