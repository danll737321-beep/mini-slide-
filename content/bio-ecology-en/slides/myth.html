COMMON MISCONCEPTION

Arrows show where the energy goes

Misconception

Wolf → Rabbit → Grass

The arrow points to what gets eaten.

Correct

Grass → Rabbit → Wolf

The arrow follows the energy: from the eaten to the eater.

Ecology: Food Webs & Energy Pyramids · 5

Tip: read each arrow as “is eaten by” or “energy flows to.” Grass is eaten by the rabbit; the rabbit is eaten by the wolf. When students draw their webs, have them check that every arrow points up from the producers.
