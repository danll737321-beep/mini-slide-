THE MOST COMMON MYTH

Do arrows point to what gets eaten?

No.

Arrows show the flow of energy: from the food to the eater. So it’s grass → rabbit → wolf, not wolf → rabbit → grass.

Read every arrow as “is eaten by” and you’ll never get it backward.

Ecology: Food Webs & Energy Pyramids · 12

When students draw their own webs, have them check that every arrow points up from the producers.
