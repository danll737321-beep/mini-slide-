ACTIVITY · 8–13 MIN · CIRCLE OF 8

Build a food web with yarn

  1. Eight students each take a role card and stand in a circle.
  2. Pass the yarn from each organism to whatever eats it.
  3. The teacher says “The wolf is gone.” The wolf lets go.
  4. Who feels the yarn change? Describe the chain reaction.

Ecology: Food Webs & Energy Pyramids · 6

Suggested links: Sun → Grass; Grass → Rabbit, Deer, Mouse; Rabbit → Snake, Hawk, Wolf; Mouse → Snake, Hawk; Deer → Wolf; Snake → Hawk. When the wolf lets go, the Deer student feels the yarn go slack. Guide students to say: no wolves → more deer → grass and trees get eaten. Students not in the circle draw the same web at their desks.
