BEFORE YOU START

Four words you need first

Producer

Makes its own food from sunlight, like grass and trees.

Consumer

Gets energy by eating other living things, like rabbits and wolves.

Decomposer

Breaks down dead things and returns nutrients to the soil: bacteria, fungi.

Food web

Many food chains linked together, because most animals eat more than one thing.

Ecology: Food Webs & Energy Pyramids · 4

Decomposers are often forgotten; remind students they break down every organism in the web once it dies.
