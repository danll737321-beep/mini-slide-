VOCABULARY · 2–4 MIN

Give one example of each

producer

Makes its own food, like grass or trees

consumer

Gets energy by eating other living things, like rabbits or wolves

decomposer

Breaks down dead organisms, like bacteria and fungi

food web

Many food chains linked together

Ecology: Food Webs & Energy Pyramids · 3

Ask students to give one example of their own for each word. Decomposers are often forgotten; remind students they break down every organism in the web once it dies.
