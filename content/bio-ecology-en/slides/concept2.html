One missing link changes the whole web

Yellowstone, 1920s to 1995

No wolves → elk had no predator → elk numbers boomed.

More elk → young trees eaten → riverbanks washed away.

Wolves returned in 1995, and the trees began to recover.

Ecology: Food Webs & Energy Pyramids · 7

Scientists still debate how much of the recovery is due to wolves alone, but the chain reaction is a classic example of a food web at work.
