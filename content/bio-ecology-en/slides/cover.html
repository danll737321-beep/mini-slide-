Ecology: Food Webs & Energy Pyramids

Who eats whom, and where the energy goes

Objective: Students draw a food web with at least 5 organisms and use the 10% rule to calculate energy transfer.

Prep: 8 role cards (Sun, Grass, Rabbit, Deer, Mouse, Snake, Hawk, Wolf); one ball of yarn; a map or sketch of Yellowstone National Park.
