WORKED EXAMPLE

A car goes from 0 to 20 m/s in 5 s, then cruises at 20 m/s for 5 s.

1

Acceleration (first 5 s)

a = 20 ÷ 5
  = 4 m/s²

2

Distance: triangle

½ × 5 × 20
= 50 m

3

Distance: rectangle

5 × 20
= 100 m

Total distance in 10 s: 50 + 100 = 150 m
