NEWTON’S THIRD LAW

Every push gets pushed back

Push a friend on skates: you both slide apart.

When you push something, it pushes back on you just as hard, in the opposite direction.

Forces come in pairs: equal size, opposite direction, on two different objects.

Example: a rocket pushes gas down, and the gas pushes the rocket up.

Force & Motion · 8

Other examples: when you walk, your foot pushes the ground backward and the ground pushes you forward. When you swim, you push water back and the water pushes you ahead.
