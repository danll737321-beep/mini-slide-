NEWTON’S FIRST LAW

Inertia: things keep doing what they’re doing

Pull the card out fast: the cup stays put.

Objects are “lazy.” A still object stays still. A moving object keeps going at the same speed in a straight line.

No net force → no change in motion

Myth: a moving object needs a constant push.
Fact: a hockey puck glides on its own. It only stops because friction slows it down.
