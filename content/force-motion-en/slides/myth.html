THE MOST COMMON MYTH

Do heavier objects fall faster?

No.

A heavy object gets a bigger pull from gravity, but it is also harder to speed up (F = ma). The two effects cancel out.

Without air resistance, a hammer and a feather land together. Astronauts showed this on the Moon in 1971.

Force & Motion · 12

On Earth a feather falls slowly only because of air resistance. Try it in class: drop a flat sheet of paper and a book (paper falls slowly), then crumple the paper into a ball and drop both again. They land at almost the same time. The Moon demonstration was by Apollo 15 astronaut David Scott.
