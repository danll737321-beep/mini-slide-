A COMMON MIX-UP

Force pair or balanced forces?

CompareAction–reaction pairBalanced forces
Acts onTwo different objectsOne object
Same size?AlwaysYes, when balanced
Cancel out?No (different objects)Yes, net force = 0
ExampleYou push the wall, the wall pushes youGravity pulls a book down, the table holds it up

Quick test: ask “which object does each force act on?” Two objects = a pair. One object = balanced.

Force & Motion · 9

The book on a table is the classic trap. Gravity and the table’s push both act on the book, so they are balanced forces, not an action–reaction pair. The book’s reaction partner to gravity is the book pulling Earth up.
