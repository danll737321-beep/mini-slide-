BEFORE YOU START

Four words you need first

Speed

How far you go each second. 10 m/s means 10 meters every second.

Acceleration

How much speed changes each second. 0 → 10 → 20 m/s is 10 m/s².

Force

A push or a pull, measured in newtons (N). Holding an apple takes about 1 N.

Net force

All the forces added up. 5 N left + 5 N right = 0 N.
