START WITH A PUZZLE

Why do you lurch forward when the bus brakes?

The bus stops

The brakes push on the bus and slow it down. Nothing pushes you backward.

Your body keeps going

Your body was moving at the bus’s speed, and it wants to keep moving.

No one shoved you. That feeling has a name, and it’s the first of three rules we’ll learn today.

Force & Motion · 2

Ask students first: what pushed you forward? Most will say “the bus” or “the brakes.” The surprise is that nothing pushed you forward at all. Your body simply kept moving. That is inertia, Newton’s first law.
